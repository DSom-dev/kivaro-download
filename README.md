KIVARO

Your workouts. Your progress. Your rank.

KIVARO is a fitness app built to make working out more structured, trackable, and motivating.

Track your workouts, keep your progress in one place, and build consistency as you train.

Installation

1. Download the APK above.
2. Open the downloaded file on your Android device.
3. If Android asks for permission to install from an unknown source, allow it for your browser or file manager.
4. Install KIVARO and you're ready to go.

✨ What KIVARO offers

- 🏋️ Track your workouts and exercises
- 📊 Keep a record of your training progress
- 🔥 Build and maintain workout streaks
- ⚡ Earn XP as you train
- 🏆 Progress through KIVARO's rank system
- 🏠 Gym and Home workout options
- ⏱️ Built-in workout tracking and rest timer
- 🎯 Personalized workout experience
